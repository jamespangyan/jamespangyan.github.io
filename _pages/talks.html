Industry Experience

Selected roles and contributions from my industry career.

IBM Corporation

April 2009–April 2017

  • Chief Architect (Client Technical Advisor), IBM AP Public Sector · March 2013–April 2017
  • Lead Architect and Senior Manager, IBM R&D Labs · May 2011–March 2013
  • Advisory Architect, IBM Software Labs · April 2009–April 2011

Led architecture and solution design for analytics and optimization across public-sector services, manufacturing and supply chains.

  • Designed the IBM Business Analytics Reference Architecture and provided technical leadership for ASEAN public-sector projects, including Singapore's National Electronic Health Record, Customs New TradeNet and TradeXchange, and MSF SSNet.
  • Led analytics and optimization development and services at IBM China Labs, overseeing more than 150 developers, engineers and architects.

Motorola Labs

Research Scientist · April 2007–March 2009
Motorola Electronics Pte Ltd, Singapore

Developed optimization solutions for Motorola's manufacturing and integrated supply-chain operations.

  • Developed a surface-mount technology line scheduling system that improved throughput by 15% and reduced scheduling lead time from hours to minutes.
  • Developed inventory optimization models that reduced overall inventory by 20%.

Micron Technology (Tech Semiconductor)

Senior Staff Engineer, Manufacturing Science · May 2006–March 2007
Singapore

Designed planning and scheduling solutions using optimization and queuing theory for an advanced 300 mm DRAM wafer fabrication facility.

  • Reduced wafer fabrication cycle time from approximately 110 days to 70 days.
  • Increased wafer fabrication throughput by 3–5%.
